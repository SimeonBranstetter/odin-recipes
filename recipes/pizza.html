Pepperoni Pizza

Description

A basic, circular pizza with dough, tomato based sauce, mozzarella cheese, and pepperonis, baked to perfection in an oven for your enjoyment.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough (handmade or store bought)
  • Pizza sauce (or some tomato based sauce)
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperonis
  • Italian seasoning (optional but recommended)
  • Garlic (optional)
  • Butter (optional)
