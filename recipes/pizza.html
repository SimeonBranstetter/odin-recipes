Pepperoni Pizza

Description

A basic, circular pizza with dough, tomato based sauce, mozzarella cheese, and pepperonis, baked to perfection in an oven for your enjoyment.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough (handmade or store bought)
  • Pizza sauce (or some tomato based sauce)
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperonis
  • Italian seasoning (or seasoning of choice)
  • Garlic
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Melt some butter in a glass dish or microwaveable plastic bowl (enough to spread over the dough later).
  3. Chop up garlic fine and add to melted butter; heat it again for about 15-30 seconds to get the garlic combined with the butter.
  4. Roll out pizza dough on counter or cutting board. To keep from sticking to the counter/board, use flour or a basic, low flavor profile oil.
  5. Spread the garlic butter from before evenly on the pizza dough.
  6. Insert dough into oven on bake for about 3 to 5 minutes or until dough just barely begins to brown; preferably just before.
  7. Remove pizza dough from oven and set back on counter/cutting board. Be careful as it is hot.
  8. Using a spoon or spatula, spread the pizza sauce (or tomato based sauce of choice) evenly on pizza dough.
  9. Grate mozzarella cheese onto pizza.
  10. Add pepperonis to pizza.
  11. Sprinkle on some Italian seasoning for extra flavor.
  12. Put pizza back into oven and let it cook for 10 to 20 minutes depending on the size of the pizza or until edges of crust begin to properly brown.
  13. Take pizza out of oven, cut into slices and enjoy! :D
