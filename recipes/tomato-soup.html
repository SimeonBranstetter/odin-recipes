Tomato soup

Description

A very basic bowl of tomato soup with garnish. Nothing fancy here.

Ingredients

  • Can of tomato soup
  • Salt
  • Garnish of choice

Steps

  1. Open the can of tomato soup and pour into bowl.
  2. Add some water (preferably not tap water unless said water is clean) to the soup.
  3. Put lid on top of bowl and microwave for 2 minutes.
  4. Take bowl out and give it a stir, add some salt for taste.
  5. Repeat microwave process if not hot enough but for only 1:00-1:30 minutes as to not boil over the soup.
  6. Once soup is hot enough add garnish of choice and enjoy! :0

I told you this one was nothing fancy...
