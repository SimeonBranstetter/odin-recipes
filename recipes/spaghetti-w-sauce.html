Spaghetti with sauce

Description

A simple spaghetti dish with a sauce of choice (tomato based for this recipe) and some parmesan for extra flavor.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti
  • Salt
  • Tomato based sauce (or sauce of choice)
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Turn your stovetop on high (or less if not using electric burners).
  2. Grab a large metal pot (half gallon or so will do, make sure it is not short!).
  3. Fill the pot about 1/4 to 1/2 full depending on size of pot. (remember, more water means it will take longer to boil!).
  4. Set the pot onto the burner and wait for the water to come to a rolling boil.
  5. Grab another pot (2 or 3 quarts) and set on stovetop.
  6. Add sauce of choice into 2nd pot (tomato based for this recipe) and turn burner onto medium.
  7. Sprinkle in some italian seasoning to the sauce for extra flavor.
  8. Once the first pot comes to a rolling boil, add some salt for seasoning, and then the spaghetti noodles into the pot.
  9. Keep watch of the pot as the spaghetti beings to soften. Make sure to stir often at the start so the noodles don't stick together.
  10. Keep an eye on the sauce in the 2nd pot and give it a stir every now and then to speed up heating and reduce the risk od burning. If it starts to boil turn it down slightly. When the sauce is hot enough lower the burner to simmer or take it off the burner and keep the lid on to keep it warm.
  11. Check the noodles every now and then, once they become al dente take them off the burner to stop the boiling process.
  12. grab a strainer/colander and strain the spaghetti into it. Give it a good shake to make sure all the water gets out.
  13. Grab the spaghetti and put onto plate, then the sauce that was heated up earlier.
  14. Finally grate some parmesan cheese on top and enjoy! :3
